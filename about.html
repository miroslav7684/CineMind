✦ ABOUT CINEMIND

Finding something to watch shouldn't be difficult.

CineMind is a movie and TV discovery platform designed to help users quickly find content based on their interests, mood and personal preferences.

THE PROJECT

What is CineMind?

CineMind combines real movie and television data with a custom-built Smart Search recommendation system.

Instead of browsing through hundreds of titles, users can describe what they want in normal language and CineMind analyzes those requirements to find suitable results.

Movie data, posters, ratings, backdrops and other information are loaded dynamically from TMDB.
